Who is Sougui?

Sougui is an innovative Tunisian e-commerce platform that facilitates commercial exchanges between individuals and businesses. Its name, inspired by the Arabic word «سوق» (market), reflects its core mission: creating a vibrant, accessible, and trustworthy digital marketplace for all Tunisians.

The platform offers an intuitive user experience allowing sellers to list their products easily, and buyers to find what they need in just a few clicks — from electronics and fashion to real estate and services.
